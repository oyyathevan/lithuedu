Why this page exists

The core syllabus gives you a strong foundation — 32 topics covering the essentials of Design Thinking and Innovation. But professional design practice draws on many more frameworks, models, and methods.

This section collects 35 additional topics across five unit pages. Each is written in the same style as your unit notes — with definitions, examples, and links back to the syllabus.

✅ When to read this

Read if you're preparing for a design interview, writing a portfolio, or curious about how the field extends. Skip if you're cramming for an exam — the core units already cover everything your exam asks.
